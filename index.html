Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 11, 2026

PDF Master ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

1. Document Processing & Local Storage

PDF Master is a utility and document productivity application. All PDF processing, merging, splitting, digital signing, and file conversions are performed locally on your device. We do not upload or store your personal documents on remote third-party servers without your explicit action.

2. Device Permissions Used

  • Camera: Used exclusively for capturing physical documents via the built-in Document Scanner feature to convert them into PDF.
  • Storage / Media: Used to read user-selected files/images and save generated PDF documents to your device storage.
  • Notifications: Used to deliver important application updates, feature announcements, and relevant alerts via push notifications.

3. Third-Party Services

We use trusted third-party service providers to support application functionality:

  • OneSignal & Google Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM): Used for delivering push notifications. These services may collect unique device identifiers (Device ID) solely for push delivery purposes.

4. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security practices to ensure your data and files remain private and secure.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted directly on this page.
