Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 11, 2026

PDF Master ("we", "our", or "us") values and respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines our principles and practices regarding data handling when you use the PDF Master mobile application.

Strict Local Processing Guarantee: All document modifications, conversions, signatures, and scans are performed entirely on your local device. We never upload your personal document files to remote servers.

1. Document Processing & Local Storage

PDF Master is an offline-first productivity tool. When you edit, merge, split, compress, or convert documents:

  • Your files remain stored exclusively on your local device storage.
  • We do not collect, read, analyze, or transfer your PDF contents or personal documents.

2. Device Permissions & Purpose

To provide full functionality, PDF Master requests the following device permissions:

  • Camera Access: Required strictly for the built-in Document Scanner feature to capture physical documents and convert them into digital PDFs.
  • Storage / Media Files: Required to open user-selected documents/photos and save the resulting processed PDF files directly to your device storage.
  • Push Notifications: Used to deliver non-intrusive service announcements, new feature updates, and productivity reminders.

3. Third-Party Service Providers

We work with industry-standard third-party services for push notification delivery:

  • OneSignal & Google Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM): Used exclusively to deliver push notifications. These services process anonymous, unique device identifiers (Device Tokens) solely for message routing.

4. Children's Privacy

Our application does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children.

5. Data Security

We implement rigorous security standards to safeguard the integrity of the application. Because your documents are processed locally, your sensitive information remains under your direct control at all times.
